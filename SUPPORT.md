# Support

cudaq-pulse is research-preview software. It is not an NVIDIA product and is
not a supported feature of CUDA-Q: there is no service-level agreement, and
no guarantee of a response, a fix, or compatibility between releases beyond
the [versioning policy](CONTRIBUTING.md#versioning-and-stability).

- **Questions and bugs**: open a
  [GitHub issue](https://github.com/NVIDIA/cudaq-pulse/issues) with the
  output of `python -m cudaq_pulse.doctor`. The maintainers answer on a
  best-effort basis.
- **Security vulnerabilities**: follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md); do not
  open an issue.
- **Documentation**: the [user guide](https://nvidia.github.io/cudaq-pulse/)
  and the [examples](examples/).

Only the latest release is maintained. Fixes land on `main` and ship in the
next release; earlier releases are not patched.
